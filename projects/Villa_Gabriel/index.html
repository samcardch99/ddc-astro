Villa Gabriel

6650 SW 94th Ave, Miami, FL

4 Bedrooms ● 4 Bathrooms

A contemporary estate with classical poise. The arrival sequence is defined by a soaring, double-height portico wrapped in warm vertical wood and soft arch lighting, setting a calm, elegant tone. Inside, an open great room flows to the dining area and chef’s kitchen with sculpted stone surfaces, fluted wood detailing, and custom millwork. The primary suite is a private retreat with a spa-style bath and tailored dressing room, while three secondary suites are organized for privacy. Large sliders open to a resortlike pool terrace lined with rhythmic columns, lounge areas, and lush landscaping, perfect for effortless entertaining and everyday serenity.
