Our Process

Investor-Hub
1Market analysis
We validate demand, pricing, and absorption in the target area, and review zoning, utilities, and site risks. The outcome is a clear go/no-go with a preliminary schedule and cost window.
2Financial & Loan Management
We assemble the lender package, secure terms with regional banks, and coordinate appraisal and closing. During the build, we manage draw schedules, inspections, and compliance so funds flow on time.
3Architectural project
We develop the design aligned to the panelized steel system—walls, roof, and floor—defining service paths, openings, and connection details. You receive permit-ready plans, panel schedules, and a crane/assembly plan.
4Fast & Ecological Modular Construction
Panels are fabricated with Florida partners, labeled for traceability, and delivered to site for a repeatable assembly sequence: layout → first lift → lock & align → close-in. Quality checks are documented at each stage until punchlist close-out.
5Pre-sale & exclusive sales
With Tsalach Real Estate, we set the go-to-market plan—positioning, pricing, and launch calendar—then manage leads, reservations, and closings. Buyers get clear documentation and timelines from the first contact to handover.
