Danilo Dominguez
CEO and Founder

Our CEO, Danilo Dominguez, has been dedicating his life to better understanding how we can better connect all real estate industries. From Real Estate Brokerage to Residential & Commercial construction. For him, it's all about the clients' experiences.

Danilo Dominguez Catasus
Chief Operating Officer

A veteran builder, Danilo turns strategy into flawless execution. He fine-tunes procurement, scheduling, and on-site workflows so our modular builds rise faster and safer. His hands-on leadership safeguards cost, quality, and compliance, delivering the operational excellence clients and investors expect from DDC.

Alejandro Gomez
CEO Executive Assistant

Alejandro is the right hand of our CEO, managing schedules, communications, and strategic priorities with precision. He ensures that every decision and initiative is executed seamlessly, supporting the leadership team in driving DDC’s vision forward.

Javi Ferrer
Sales Director

Javi turns ideas into stories people remember. Using clear data and fresh creativity, he builds campaigns that show why DDC’s modular projects stand out. From a social-post to an investor pitch, he keeps every detail polished so clients and partners feel the distinction and quality that define DDC.

Alex Olivares
Senior General Contractor

Alex is the on‑site force behind DDC’s precision builds. Drawing on deep field experience, he orchestrates crews, suppliers, and safety protocols so our modular structures rise flawlessly. His eye for detail and proactive problem‑solving keep timelines tight and quality uncompromised—delivering the craft clients expect.

Liliana Patiño
Transaction Coordinator

Liliana ensures every real estate transaction runs smoothly from start to finish. With strong attention to detail and client coordination, he bridges the gap between contracts, timelines, and closing processes. His commitment to clarity and execution makes him a key player in delivering seamless deals at DDC.

Lorena Ardila
Marketing Planner

Lorena leads the strategic coordination of our marketing efforts, ensuring every campaign aligns with DDC’s vision and brand identity. With a sharp eye for planning and a deep understanding of audience dynamics, she transforms ideas into structured actions that drive visibility, positioning, and growth. Her work connects every message with purpose.

Wilfredo Alvarado
Creative Director

Wilfredo turns concepts into immersive realities. As the mind behind DDC’s photorealistic renders, augmented-reality previews, and graphic identity, he lets clients walk through a project—feel its light, materials, and flow—long before construction begins, elevating every decision with clarity, beauty, and technical precision.

Esteban Villavicencio
Senior Civil Engineer

Esteban turns vision into buildable, code-compliant reality. He leads the civil scope—from foundations, grading, and drainage to structural coordination for modular installs—so every project meets Florida Building Code and local requirements. Working closely with our COO, Project Manager, and Senior General Contractor, he streamlines submittals, inspections, and RFIs, value-engineering connections and site works to keep timelines tight without compromising safety or quality.

Anisleidy Valle
Human Resources Leader

Anisleidy builds a strong people culture at DDC. She leads hiring, onboarding, and compliance, and supports the teams that use modular systems to deliver predictable schedules and high quality results.

Rosario Gomez
Operations Manager

Rosario ensures every operation and real estate project runs smoothly from start to finish. With a strong focus on planning and control, she connects teams, processes, and execution, playing a key role in keeping DDC’s operations efficient, coordinated, and consistently reliable.

Mateo Correa
Finance Manager

Mateo strengthens DDC’s financial operations through accounting management, treasury oversight, and the coordination of disbursements with financial institutions. With a focus on efficiency and control, he helps optimize processes, ensure resource availability, and support key decision making, contributing to stronger results across every project.

Jorge H. Correa
Financial Analyst

Jorge brings analytical rigor to every financial decision at DDC. He builds project budgets, cash-flow projections, and cost analyses, tracking the variance between what was estimated and what is actually spent. By turning numbers into clear insight, he supports investment evaluation, margin control, and the reporting that keeps each development financially sound.

Daniela Marichal
Permit Runner

Daniela is the vital link between our projects and local authorities. She navigates the complexities of municipal bureaucracy with speed and precision, ensuring all permits and documentation are processed without delay. Her proactive coordination keeps our construction timelines on track, allowing DDC to maintain its commitment to rapid and compliant modular delivery.

Dany Rodriguez
Superintendent

Dany brings a keen, vigilant perspective to the construction site, transforming blueprints into physical structures with unwavering attention to detail. His watchful eyes oversee every trade, ensuring that the highest standards of quality are maintained at every phase.
